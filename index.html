Where it all quietly began

Where an Unexpected Hello Became Something Special 🌙📖

Some friendships do not arrive with a grand introduction. Sometimes they begin with one ordinary question.

The first hello

“Just another classmate… at first.” 🌱

At the beginning, you were simply someone from the same class — a familiar face, but still a stranger. Then came a small academic doubt, a quick conversation, and that tiny moment when two people who barely knew each other started noticing each other.

A little more every day

“Somehow, our conversations kept growing.” 📚

Study topics, assignments, exams, projects and all those little academic doubts became reasons to talk. Soon, the conversations were no longer only about college. A few words became longer talks, and a classmate slowly became someone familiar.

From familiar to friends

“Then talking started feeling easy.” ☕

There was no need for a reason anymore. Random jokes, small updates, teasing, helping each other, sharing thoughts and simply being there — somewhere along the way, the awkwardness disappeared. We were not just classmates anymore. We had become friends.

Where we are now

“A stranger became someone I never want to lose.” ✨

That is the part I value most. What started as an ordinary connection slowly became a friendship filled with memories, comfort and genuine care. Now you are not just someone I met in a classroom — you are one of those people whose happiness, success and smile truly matter to me.
