# Surya Birthday Happiness Website

Open `index.html` in a modern browser.

## Included
- Dark magical birthday theme
- Friendship-first opening and poem
- Story that begins with an unknown person and grows through academic questions into a close friendship
- Local image assets for the friendship/magical memory sections
- One local MP4 video at `assets/videos/doremon.mp4`
- Video auto-plays when visible (browser autoplay rules may still require a first tap/click on some devices); controls are always enabled
- Click **Open your letter** to reveal the birthday letter
- Candle replaces the cake; click it to make the wish and trigger confetti
- Final gift reveal
- Footer: `the endless journey by vishy`
